## 📖 Project Overview

This project was completed as **Week 4 – Performance Evaluation and Strategic Recommendations** under the Yuva Intern Junior Data Analyst – Agribusiness Virtual Internship.

The project evaluates a three-year hypothetical dataset covering:

- FY 2023–24
- FY 2024–25
- FY 2025–26

It examines the performance of an onion-producing and aggregation enterprise across the agricultural value chain, from cultivation and irrigation to post-harvest management, logistics and wholesale marketing.

The primary objective is to identify operational strengths, evaluate performance gaps, compare results against relevant benchmarks and develop actionable recommendations for improving agribusiness performance.

## 🌾 Business Scenario

The report considers a hypothetical onion-producing enterprise operating in Maharashtra, India.

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| Business Model | Farmer-linked onion production, aggregation and wholesale marketing |
| Geographic Focus | Maharashtra, India |
| Farmer Network | Approximately 500 farmers |
| Cultivated Area | Approximately 1,000 hectares |
| Evaluation Period | FY 2023–24 to FY 2025–26 |
| Industry | Agriculture and Agribusiness |
| Data Type | Hypothetical and illustrative |

The enterprise's value chain includes:

1. Agricultural input coordination
2. Onion cultivation
3. Harvesting
4. Curing and grading
5. Storage and inventory management
6. Transportation and logistics
7. Wholesale marketing

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Evaluate agribusiness performance using measurable KPIs.
- Analyse production efficiency and financial profitability.
- Assess water-use productivity and operational sustainability.
- Identify post-harvest losses and supply-chain inefficiencies.
- Compare performance against external references and internal management targets.
- Develop practical, data-supported strategic recommendations.
- Establish an ongoing monitoring and evaluation framework.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators

Seven critical KPIs were selected to evaluate the enterprise.

| KPI | Measurement | Desired Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Harvested Yield | Gross harvested tonnes ÷ harvested hectares | Increase |
| Operating Profit Margin | Operating profit ÷ operating revenue × 100 | Increase |
| Water Productivity | Harvested output (kg) ÷ irrigation water applied (m³) | Increase |
| Post-Harvest Loss | Physical loss weight ÷ gross harvested weight × 100 | Decrease |
| On-Time Delivery | Orders delivered within agreed window ÷ eligible orders × 100 | Increase |
| Cost per Tonne | Operating cost ÷ gross harvested tonnes | Decrease |
| Marketable Production Rate | Saleable-grade output ÷ gross harvested weight × 100 | Increase |

## 📈 Key Findings

The following table presents the hypothetical enterprise's three-year performance.

| KPI | FY 2023–24 | FY 2024–25 | FY 2025–26 | Internal Target |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Harvested Yield (t/ha) | 15.8 | 16.5 | 17.2 | 15.5 |
| Operating Profit Margin (%) | 18.3 | 23.7 | 28.5 | 25% |
| Water Productivity (kg/m³) | 5.2 | 5.6 | 6.1 | 6.5 |
| Post-Harvest Loss (%) | 14.5 | 12.8 | 11.5 | 9% |
| On-Time Delivery (%) | 78 | 84 | 88 | 95% |
| Cost per Tonne (₹) | 14,700 | 14,500 | 14,300 | ₹13,500 |
| Marketable Production (%) | 82.0 | 85.0 | 88.5 | 95% |

### Major Observations

**1. Production Efficiency**

Harvested yield increased from 15.8 to 17.2 tonnes per hectare, representing an improvement of approximately 8.9%.

**2. Financial Performance**

Operating profit margin increased from 18.3% to 28.5%, showing an improvement of 10.2 percentage points.

**3. Water Productivity**

Water productivity improved from 5.2 to 6.1 kg/m³. However, it remains below the internal target of 6.5 kg/m³.

**4. Post-Harvest Losses**

Physical post-harvest losses declined from 14.5% to 11.5%, but further improvements are required to achieve the 9% target.

**5. Supply-Chain Performance**

On-time delivery improved from 78% to 88%, indicating better logistics performance, although it remains below the 95% target.

**6. Cost Efficiency**

Cost per tonne declined from ₹14,700 to ₹14,300. However, the enterprise still has an opportunity to reduce operating costs.

**7. Marketable Production**

Marketable production increased from 82% to 88.5%, indicating improved quality and saleability.

## 🔍 Methodology

The report follows a structured data analysis and performance evaluation process.

### 1. Data Collection

Potential data sources include:

- Government agricultural statistics
- AGMARKNET market price and arrival records
- ICAR research publications
- Farm production registers
- Harvest and weighbridge records
- Water-meter readings
- Grading and rejection records
- Sales invoices
- Transportation and logistics records

### 2. Data Cleaning and Preparation

The methodology includes:

- Identifying missing values.
- Removing duplicate transaction records.
- Standardising measurement units.
- Checking inconsistent dates and quantities.
- Validating farmer and plot identifiers.
- Reconciling harvested, stored and dispatched quantities.

### 3. KPI Calculation

Each KPI is calculated using a predefined formula and consistent measurement units.

### 4. Trend Analysis

Three-year performance is compared to identify improvements, declining performance and operational gaps.

### 5. Benchmark Analysis

Performance is evaluated using:

- Government agricultural statistics.
- Relevant research publications.
- Internal management targets.

External statistical benchmarks are distinguished from internal planning targets.

### 6. Strategic Evaluation

The findings are converted into practical recommendations with measurable outcomes, responsible owners and implementation timelines.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

The report proposes seven strategic improvement areas.

| Area | Recommendation | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Post-Harvest Management | Improve curing, handling, ventilation and storage monitoring | Reduce physical losses |
| Irrigation Efficiency | Introduce metered irrigation and improved scheduling | Improve water productivity |
| Quality Management | Standardise grading and quality inspection | Increase marketable production |
| Logistics | Improve harvest forecasting and dispatch planning | Increase on-time delivery |
| Market Intelligence | Monitor market prices, arrivals and grade-wise realisation | Protect profitability |
| Cost Management | Analyse operational expenses by activity | Reduce cost per tonne |
| KPI Monitoring | Establish a centralised performance dashboard | Improve accountability |

Recommendations are designed to be tested through pilot implementations before wider expansion.

## 🗓️ Implementation Roadmap

An 18-month implementation roadmap is proposed.

| Timeline | Main Activities |
|---|---|
| Month 1 | Establish KPI definitions, data ownership and reporting procedures |
| Months 2–3 | Clean historical records, validate equipment and prepare pilot programmes |
| Months 4–6 | Implement irrigation, grading, post-harvest and logistics pilots |
| Months 7–9 | Evaluate pilot outcomes and validate financial benefits |
| Months 10–12 | Expand successful interventions |
| Months 13–15 | Conduct performance audits and corrective actions |
| Months 16–18 | Complete impact evaluation and prepare the next operating plan |

Performance is monitored through daily, weekly, monthly, seasonal, quarterly and annual reviews.

## ⚠️ Data Quality and Risk Management

The report identifies several potential sources of analytical bias and operational risk.

### Potential Data Issues

- Selection bias
- Coverage bias
- Measurement errors
- Reporting and recall bias
- Seasonal and weather-related variations
- Price and grade-mix effects
- Incorrect KPI denominators
- Missing records
- Aggregation bias
- Double counting of post-harvest losses

### Risk Management

The proposed risk-management framework considers:

- Extreme rainfall and drought
- Storage deterioration
- Irrigation system failures
- Market price volatility
- Transportation disruptions
- Poor data quality
- Low farmer adoption
- Operational cost overruns

Each risk is assigned an accountable role, warning indicators and mitigation measures.
